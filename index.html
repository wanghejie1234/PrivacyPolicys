1. Introduction

Wantgame ("we", "us", or "our") develops and provides Woofdoku: Panda Logic Puzzle (the "Game") for Android. This Privacy Policy describes the information that may be handled through the Game and the third-party services integrated into it.

The Game's intended audience includes children. This makes children's privacy and age-appropriate processing particularly important. Some implementation details described below are pending verification; no unverified safeguard is represented as already active.

2. Information that may be processed

The Game uses Google AdMob, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play Games Services. Depending on the relevant service and its configuration, these integrations may process:

  • Device and app information: operating system, device model, app version, performance details, technical identifiers, and installation-related identifiers.
  • Game and service activity: app launches, sessions, interactions, gameplay events reported to analytics, achievements, leaderboard scores, and saved game progress.
  • Diagnostics: crash reports, stack traces, app state, device information at the time of a crash, and crash-related installation identifiers.
  • Advertising-related information: IP address (which can indicate an approximate location), ad interactions, diagnostic information, and certain identifiers. Whether advertising identifiers are transmitted in the released Game depends on its configuration and remains unverified.
  • Google Play Games information: a Play Games player identifier or profile details used by Google Play Games Services, together with achievements, leaderboard participation, and cloud-saved game information, as applicable.

The Game does not offer a separate Wantgame account. Google Play Games sign-in is a third-party sign-in feature and does not mean that Wantgame operates a separate registration system.

The categories listed above describe service capabilities and commonly processed data, rather than a claim that every category is transmitted for every player. The final disclosure must be checked against the actual app build and SDK settings.

3. Purposes of processing

Depending on the enabled SDK functions, information may be used to:

  • Provide Play Games sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, and cloud game saves.
  • Understand aggregate game usage through Firebase Analytics.
  • Identify and investigate crashes and stability issues through Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Request and display advertisements through Google AdMob, subject to the game's actual ad settings.
  • Maintain service functionality and help detect abuse or technical problems, where supported by the service.
